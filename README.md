# My favorite dam : Masjed soleyman earth dam
# Introduction
The Masjed Soleyman Dam, also known as the Godar-e Landar Dam, is a major hydroelectric powerplant located in Khuzestan Province, Iran. 
The facility is situated on the Karun river and serves as a critical component of the region's energy infrastructure.
The Masjed Soleyman Dam is engineered as a rock-fill structure featuring a vertical clay-core, a design choice optimized for the geological conditions along the Karun river in Iran
